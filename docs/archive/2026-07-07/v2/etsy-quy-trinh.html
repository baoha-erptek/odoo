Kho khan hien tai

10 diem dau toan doi dang gap

  • Copy/paste thu cong vao portal Gearment — de nham, mat thoi gian
  • Ma tracking chep lai tay vao Sheet — hay bi sot, bi sai
  • File thiet ke >20MB khong gui duoc qua Discord — bi that lac
  • Ton kho nguyen lieu kiem tra thu cong moi thang — hay thieu bat ngo
  • Khong co he thong hoan/doi hang — xu ly roi rac, khong luu lich su
  • Trang thai don hang phai cap nhat thu cong — de quen, de sai

Vai tro moi

Moi nguoi lam gi trong quy trinh sau Odoo

MP — Marketing

  • Xem danh sach don trong Odoo
  • Duyet file thiet ke (click trong he thong)
  • Khong can cap nhat Sheet thu cong

BA — Van hanh

  • Lam file thiet ke, tai len Odoo
  • Mua nhan ship qua bang quan ly
  • Khong can copy-paste tracking, chep Sheet

PD — San xuat

  • Nhan Lenh san xuat tu Odoo
  • Bam "Hoan thanh" khi xong
  • Kho tu dong cap nhat, khong can bao cao tay

Kho — Nguyen lieu

  • Ton kho cap nhat real-time
  • Bao cao nhap kho qua Odoo
  • Khong con kiem tra thu cong moi thang

RD — Gia ca

  • Theo doi gia tren Odoo (tu dong)
  • Canh bao lech gia co the tu dong

⚡ He thong Odoo

  • Nhan don tu Etsy
  • Phan loai tuyen tu dong
  • Gui don Gearment, tao Lenh SX
  • Cap nhat tracking
